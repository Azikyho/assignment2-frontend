About Azeke Industries

Who We Are

Azeke Industries is a bicycle store specializing in fixed-gear bikes, framesets, components, and cycling accessories. Our goal is to provide riders with high-quality products from popular and trusted cycling brands. We work with products for both experienced cyclists and people who are just starting their journey in fixed-gear and urban cycling.

Our catalog includes bicycles, framesets, wheels, handlebars, saddles, pedals, drivetrain components, and other essential parts. We focus on modern design, reliability, performance, and products that meet the needs of everyday urban riders.

Our Brands

At Azeke Industries, customers can explore products from well-known cycling brands such as Cinelli, Constantine, Aventon, Dosnoventa, LOOK and other manufacturers. We continuously update our collection with new bicycles, framesets, components, and accessories.

Our Mission

Our mission is to develop and support cycling culture by making quality bicycles and components accessible to riders. We believe that a bicycle is more than just transportation — it is a combination of performance, freedom, style, and individuality.
